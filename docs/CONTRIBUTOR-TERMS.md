# Contributor terms

Version **2026-10-24**.

These terms apply when you switch on `adk contribute` (or **Data sharing** in your
Aitherium settings) and send data to Aitherium. Nothing is sent until you switch a
category on, and you can switch it off or delete everything at any time.

## What you can share

You choose, per category: **session lessons** (what worked and what failed in your agent
sessions), **skills**, **tools**, and **agent packs**. Before anything leaves your
machine, it removes secrets, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, internal
hostnames, your user and machine names, home folder paths, tenant ids and any names on
your denylist. `adk contribute preview` shows you exactly what would be sent.

## How it may be used: your choice, per category

| tier | what Aitherium may do |
|---|---|
| **Private training** (default) | Use it to train and evaluate models. The raw contribution is never published or given to anyone else. |
| **Open, with credit** (CC BY 4.0) | Everything above, and publish it in an open dataset under CC BY 4.0, credited to the name you choose (or "an Aitherium contributor"). |
| **Public domain** (CC0 1.0) | Everything above, and publish it in an open dataset under CC0 1.0, with no credit required. |

You confirm you have the right to share what you switch on, and that it is not someone
else's confidential information.

## What you get back

Nothing is paid, credited or discounted for contributing. If that ever changes, it will be
offered as a new version of these terms that you can accept or decline.

## How long it is kept

Contributions waiting for processing or review are deleted after **90 days**. What has
already gone into a training set or open dataset stays there until you delete it.

## Deleting

`adk contribute delete` (or **Turn everything off and remove what was sent**) switches
everything off and removes your contributions from staging, review, every training set
and open dataset built so far, and every one built later. Models already trained on the
data are not retrained.

## Changes

A new version of these terms takes effect only for contributions you send after you
accept it. Until you do, nothing more is sent.
